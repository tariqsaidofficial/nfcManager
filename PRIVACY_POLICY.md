# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date**: October 17, 2025  
**Last Updated**: October 17, 2025

## 🛡️ Our Commitment to Privacy

At NFC Manager, your privacy is our top priority. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our application. We believe in complete transparency and user control over personal data.

## Information We Collect

### Information We DO NOT Collect
- **Personal Information**: We do not collect names, email addresses, or phone numbers
- **NFC Data**: We do not store or transmit NFC tag data
- **Location Data**: We do not track your location
- **Device Identifiers**: We do not collect unique device identifiers
- **Usage Analytics**: We do not track your app usage patterns

### Information We DO Collect
- **Technical Information**: Basic device information required for app functionality (Android version, NFC capability)
- **Crash Reports**: Anonymous crash logs to improve app stability (if you opt-in)

## How We Use Information

The limited technical information we access is used solely to:
- **Enable NFC functionality** on your device
- **Provide core app features** like NFC monitoring
- **Ensure app compatibility** with your device
- **Improve app stability** through crash reporting (optional)

## Data Storage and Security

- **Local Storage Only**: All app data is stored locally on your device
- **No Cloud Storage**: We do not store any data on external servers
- **No Data Transmission**: The app does not send data to external services
- **Secure Processing**: All NFC data processing happens locally and securely

## Permissions Explained

Our app requires the following permissions:

### NFC Permission
- **Purpose**: Access NFC hardware to monitor NFC events
- **Usage**: Read NFC capabilities and detect nearby NFC devices
- **Data Access**: Technical NFC status only, no sensitive data

### Foreground Service Permission
- **Purpose**: Continue monitoring NFC in the background
- **Usage**: Maintain NFC monitoring when app is not active
- **Data Access**: No additional data access

### Notification Permission (Android 13+)
- **Purpose**: Show notifications about NFC events
- **Usage**: Display monitoring status and NFC detection alerts
- **Data Access**: No data collection

## Third-Party Services

We do not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

## Children's Privacy

Our app does not target children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## Changes to Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. We will notify users of any material changes by:
- Updating the "Last Updated" date
- Posting a notice in the app (for significant changes)
- Publishing changes on our GitHub repository

## International Users

This app is designed to comply with:
- **GDPR** (European Union General Data Protection Regulation)
- **CCPA** (California Consumer Privacy Act)
- **Other applicable privacy laws**

Since we don't collect personal data, most privacy regulations don't apply to our data practices.

## Your Rights

Even though we don't collect personal data, you have the right to:
- **Access**: Request information about any data we might have
- **Deletion**: Request deletion of any data (though we don't store personal data)
- **Portability**: Request data export (not applicable as no data is stored)
- **Questions**: Contact us about our privacy practices

## Data Retention

Since we don't collect or store personal data:
- **No retention period applies**
- **Local device data** is controlled by you
- **Uninstalling the app** removes all app-related data

## Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our privacy practices, contact us:

- **Email**: support@dxbmark.com
- **Response Time**: 24-48 hours for privacy inquiries
- **Languages**: English, Arabic

## Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

Where applicable under GDPR, our legal basis for processing is:
- **Legitimate Interest**: Providing core app functionality
- **Consent**: For optional features like crash reporting

## Data Protection Officer

Given the minimal data processing, we have not appointed a Data Protection Officer. For privacy questions, contact us using the information above.
